AIDOO Whisper Control — Hey, AIDOO model notices
================================================

The custom hey_aidoo.onnx and hey_aidoo_confirmation.onnx classifiers were
trained by Aidoo Ltd. OOD with the
Apache-2.0 licensed LiveKit wake-word training tools:
https://github.com/livekit/livekit-wakeword

General-speech negatives were sampled as frozen embeddings from LiveKit's
published wake-word feature dataset. Neither the source audio nor the feature
dataset is distributed with AIDOO Whisper Control.

Synthetic training speech was produced with the MIT-licensed
rhasspy/piper-voices en_US-libritts_r-medium model:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/libritts_r/medium

That voice model's model card identifies LibriTTS-R (SLR141) as its dataset
and the dataset license as Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://www.openslr.org/141/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The generated speech was transformed with room impulse responses and
background audio, converted into frozen audio embeddings, and used to train a
two-stage wake-word classifier. AIDOO Whisper Control does not distribute the
Piper voice model or the LibriTTS-R recordings.

Dataset citation requested by the LibriTTS-R authors:

Yuma Koizumi, Heiga Zen, Shigeki Karita, Yifan Ding, Kohei Yatabe,
Nobuyuki Morioka, Michiel Bacchiani, Yu Zhang, Wei Han, and Ankur Bapna,
“LibriTTS-R: A Restored Multi-Speaker Text-to-Speech Corpus,” arXiv, 2023.
